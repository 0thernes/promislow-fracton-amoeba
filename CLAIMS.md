# CLAIMS.md — Claim Ledger

This ledger lists each mathematical, algorithmic and empirical claim of `promislow-fracton-amoeba` v1.0.0 with its verification tier, artifact and scope (Build Standard v1.2.0 B04 / Playbook v1.3). It was corrected on 2026-11-26 to match an independent statement audit ([audit log](https://github.com/0thernes/0thernes-research-archive/blob/main/docs/reviews/2026-11-26-statement-audit.md)). The earlier wording, and the errors that remain in the frozen paper, are listed in [`ERRATA.md`](ERRATA.md).

**Overall assessment.** The Lean content is a kernel-checked formalization of known facts, elementary identities and finite computations about the Hantzsche–Wendt group ring, Haah's CC1 curve and $S_4$. No result answers a published open question. Several theorem names (for example `..._hensel_tower`, `..._no_strings`, `..._ronkin_...`, `..._artin_b4_...`) describe more than the Lean statement proves; the "Claim" column below states what is proved.

## Verification Tier Definitions
- **`kernel-checked`**: Proved in zero-Mathlib Lean 4.35.0 (`lean/`), with no `sorry` in the proof files, no `native_decide`, and axioms within `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`. Checked by Lean (`lake build`, `leanchecker`); the 16 comparator targets in `lean/comparator.json` were also accepted by 6 independent kernels (`lean`, `leanchecker-paranoid`, `lean4lean`, `nanoda_bin`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`). `lean/PalomarChallenge.lean` is the comparator challenge template and intentionally contains `sorry` bodies.
- **`exact-comp`**: Computed with exact integer / finite-field arithmetic in both Rust (`rust/`) and Python (`python/pfa_verify/exact_core.py`), with differential assertions (`tests/test_all.py`). Not in Lean.
- **`numerical-approx`**: Floating-point quadrature or complex root tracking with gap bounds, grid-refinement checks and Rust↔Python agreement `< 1e-6`. Not in Lean.
- **`paper-level`**: An argument given in the paper but not formalized.
- **`open-problem`**: Not claimed solved.

## Prior Work
- Gardam, *A counterexample to the unit conjecture for group rings*, Annals of Math. 194 (2021), arXiv:2102.11818: the 21-term unit $\alpha$, $\beta$ in $\mathbb{F}_2[P]$ used throughout.
- Gardam, *Non-trivial units of complex group rings*, arXiv:2312.05240: $\mathbb{C}[P]$ has non-trivial units, so the unit conjecture is false in characteristic 0.
- Passman, *On the counterexamples to the unit conjecture for group rings*, arXiv:2108.06570: the $4 \times 4$ matrix representation of the group ring of $P$ and the determinants of units in it.
- Tabei, arXiv:2608.00103 (preprint): no unit of $\mathbb{Z}[P]$ supported in $B(4)$ has nontrivial reduction mod 2. This implies that no sign lift of Gardam's support is a unit (the `T1f` conclusion).
- Haah, arXiv:1101.1962: local stabilizer codes in 3D (including CC1) with no string logical operators. Kim, arXiv:1202.0052: sufficient conditions for the absence of string operators in qudit cubic codes. Dua–Kim–Cheng–Williamson, arXiv:1908.08049.
- Tian–Samperton–Wang, arXiv:1812.02101 (Haah codes on general 3-manifolds) and Lin–Pryadko, arXiv:2306.16400 (codes over arbitrary, generally non-abelian, finite groups): related to the CSS pairs of `T2a`/`T2e`.

## Falsification Criteria (What Would Prove Us Wrong — `B42`)

Any single independently reproducible finding below refutes the corresponding claim and triggers a public entry in [`ERRATA.md`](ERRATA.md):

1. **Formal Lean 4.35.0 Theorems (`T1a`–`T3c`):** Failure of `lake build`, `lake env leanchecker-paranoid --fresh PalomarSolution`, `lake comparator --config comparator.json --paranoid`, or any non-standard axiom outside `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` reported by `lake env lean VerifyAxioms.lean`.
2. **Exact Arithmetic Certificates (`E1`):** Any mismatch between independent Rust (`rust/src/lib.rs`) and Python (`python/pfa_verify/exact_core.py`) 2-adic inverse support sizes `[21, 169, 459, 1388, 2100, 4353]` for $m=1\dots 6$, or any CC1 code dimension $k_p(L)$ differing from `results/exact_verification_report.json`.
3. **Numerical Companions (`N1`):** Grid refinement or independent complex root-tracking showing Rust vs. Python discrepancy $\ge 10^{-6}$ on Ronkin quadrature, bipolar winding symmetry $W_x(k_y,-k_z) = -W_x(k_y,k_z)$, or quartic discriminant monodromy permutations $(1\,2)$ and $(0\,2\,1\,3)$.
