# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, rigorous, and harassment-free scholarly and technical community for everyone regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected Behavior
- Engage in constructive, evidence-based mathematical and engineering critique.
- Respect explicit verification boundaries and credit prior scholarship accurately.
- Accept peer and red-team critique gracefully when errors or gaps are uncovered.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or discriminatory remarks.
- Fabrication of citations, proof results, or experimental outputs.
