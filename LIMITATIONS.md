# LIMITATIONS.md — Scope Boundaries, Threats to Validity, and Falsification Conditions

Per Build Standard v1.2.0 (`B41`, `B42`) and Playbook v1.3, this document records the known boundaries of the results, separates formal proofs from computations, and states conditions that would falsify the claims. It was corrected on 2026-11-26 after an independent statement audit; see [`ERRATA.md`](ERRATA.md).

## 1. Scope Limits

1. **The unit conjecture over $\mathbb{C}$ is settled; over $\mathbb{Z}$ and $\mathbb{Q}$ it is open.**
   - Gardam, arXiv:2312.05240, showed that $\mathbb{C}[P]$ has non-trivial units, so the unit conjecture is false in characteristic 0. Whether $\mathbb{Z}[P]$ or $\mathbb{Q}[P]$ has non-trivial units remains open.
   - Theorem `T1f` (`t1f_exhaustive_no_sign_lift_gardam_support` and `strengthened_no_sign_lift_of_gardam_support`) shows that none of the $2^{21} = 2{,}097{,}152$ sign lifts on Gardam's 21-element support passes the 8 sign-point determinant test, also when the $p$-part is any function even at sign points. This is a necessary condition for being a unit. Lean does not prove that the lifts are non-units: that step needs determinant multiplicativity and a link between the sign-point matrix `blochAtSign` and $\Psi$, which are paper arguments. The non-unit conclusion for these lifts is implied by Tabei, arXiv:2608.00103 (preprint), which shows that no unit of $\mathbb{Z}[P]$ supported in $B(4)$ has nontrivial reduction mod 2.
   - `T1f` says nothing about units of $\mathbb{Z}[P]$ or $\mathbb{Q}[P]$ with larger supports, shifted supports, or integer coefficients outside $\{\pm 1\}$.
2. **2-adic lifting of $\widetilde{\alpha}$ is not in Lean, and it does not give a $\mathbb{Z}[P]$ unit.**
   - Invertibility of $\widetilde{\alpha}$ in $(\mathbb{Z}/2^m\mathbb{Z})[P]$ for every $m \ge 1$ is standard lifting (2 is nilpotent mod $2^m$). The theorem `t1e_gardam_unit_mod4_and_hensel_tower` contains no Hensel statement; `hensel_geometric_identity` is a generic identity that is not applied to Gardam's unit. The inverse support sizes (`21, 169, 459, 1388, 2100, 4353` for $m=1\dots 6$) are Rust/Python computations (`exact-comp`). The 2-adic limit lives in a completion, not in the finite group ring $\mathbb{Z}[P]$.
3. **Several Lean statements are narrower than their names.**
   - `T1c`: the determinant part is quantified over integer torus points, which are the 8 sign points where the $V_4$ action is the identity, so it is a tautology. Constancy of unit determinants is a paper argument.
   - `T1b`: injectivity is proved for the separate coefficient map `psiFormal`, which no lemma links to the evaluation map.
   - `T1d`, `T1e`, `T1f`: the sign-point matrix `blochAtSign` and the Laurent determinant routine (`zpBlochMat`, `lpDet4`) are defined separately from $\Psi$, with no linking lemma.
   - `T2c`: string logical operators are not defined in Lean. The hypotheses require roots of three quadratics of discriminant 5, so the statement is vacuous over $\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{Q}$ and $\mathbb{F}_p$ with $p \equiv \pm 2 \pmod 5$; characteristic 2 is covered only by one line over $\mathbb{F}_4$.
   - `T2b`, `T2d`: the map from signed codes to discriminants is a definition, and no code dimension $k_p(L)$ is defined; $k_{23}(11)=6$ and $k_3(8)=24$ are `exact-comp`.
   - `T3b`: the "amoeba" predicates are definitions of inequalities checked at integer points; no amoeba is defined.
4. **Numerical approximations in Pillar III are not interval-certified and not in Lean.**
   - The 3D Ronkin integral $R(\mu)$ (`N=16` quadrature, $R(0) \approx 4.271743$), the $25 \times 25$ spectral winding map $W_x(k_y,k_z)$ (evaluated on `npts=384` with gap filter $\min|\det| > 0.35$), and the 600–1024-step complex eigenvalue loop tracking / quartic discriminant winding degrees are floating-point computations (`numerical-approx`).
   - Because $\log|\det\Psi|$ has $1$D logarithmic singularities on $\mathbb{T}^3$ where $\det\Psi = 0$, uniform trapezoidal quadrature of $R(0)$ converges at $O(1/N)$ (`4.2292` at $N=12$ vs `4.2717` at $N=16$). What Lean proves (`T3a`) is a non-strict lemma: $R(0,0,0) \le R(\mu)$ for every midpoint-convex function $\mathbb{Q}^3 \to \mathbb{Q}$ invariant under the three double sign flips. The minimum need not be strict (constant functions satisfy the hypotheses), and no Ronkin function appears in Lean.
5. **Gottschalk's surjunctivity conjecture for general groups remains open.**
   - Theorem `T1g` gives the bijective 21-neighbor linear cellular automaton on $\mathbb{F}_2^P$ attached to Gardam's unit. $P$ is virtually abelian, hence amenable and sofic, where surjunctivity is already known, so `T1g` adds nothing toward the conjecture.
6. **The CSS pairs on $P$ are not shown to be fracton codes.**
   - Theorem `T2e` checks check-polynomial lengths `(52, 52)` / `(52, 56)`, vanishing of the commutator mod 2, the 342-term $\mathbb{Z}[P]$ commutator, and commutation of a second explicit pair. No distance, logical-qubit count or fracton property is formalized; the paper's $k(M_L) = 4\,k_{\mathrm{Haah}}(L)$ is not in Lean. `T2a` holds also for $u = 1$, so commutation does not depend on Gardam's unit. Distance scaling $d(M_L)$ on large Hantzsche–Wendt covers remains open. Related constructions exist (Tian–Samperton–Wang, arXiv:1812.02101; Lin–Pryadko, arXiv:2306.16400).
7. **$S_4$ facts in Lean vs. numerical eigenvalue paths.**
   - Theorem `T3c` proves that adjacent transpositions in $S_4$ satisfy the braid relations and that the observed permutations `[0,2,1,3]` and `[2,3,1,0]` generate all 24 elements of $S_4$. No braid group $B_4$ is defined. Linking those permutations to the matrix family $\Psi(\widetilde{\alpha})(k)$ relies on floating-point Durand–Kerner root tracking and numerical quartic discriminant winding (compared between Rust and Python, with spectral gaps $\ge 0.4841$ and raw discriminant degrees within $10^{-14}$ of integers).
8. **Prior work.** The $4 \times 4$ representation and the unit-determinant argument are discussed by Passman (arXiv:2108.06570). The qubit no-strings property of CC1 is Haah's (arXiv:1101.1962); Kim (arXiv:1202.0052) and Dua–Kim–Cheng–Williamson (arXiv:1908.08049) treat qudit and related cases. The frozen paper does not cite these; see [`ERRATA.md`](ERRATA.md).

## 2. What Would Prove Us Wrong (Falsification Conditions — B42)

Any one of the following findings would refute the corresponding claim and trigger an `ERRATA.md` entry:
1. **Lean kernel / axiom breach:** If any of the kernels run in CI (`lean`, `leanchecker`, `leanchecker-paranoid`, `lean4lean`, `nanoda_bin`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`, including `lake comparator --config comparator.json --paranoid`) rejects `PalomarSolution` or `Solution`, or if `#print axioms` reports any axiom outside `{propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound}`.
2. **Sign-lift counterexample to `T1f`:** An explicit 21-tuple of signs $c_i \in \{\pm 1\}$ on Gardam's support for which all 8 sign-point determinants of `blochAtSign` are $\pm 1$.
3. **Counterexample to the `T2c` monomial lemma:** An integral domain $K$ with $2 \neq 0$ containing roots of $P_x, P_y, P_z$, a nonzero $(a,b,c) \in \mathbb{Z}^3$ and $\lambda \in K^\times$ such that the cleared monomial identity holds for every $t \in K$. (A string-operator counterexample for CC1 would contradict the paper-level reduction, not the Lean statement.)
4. **Odd-order CC1 point for $p \le 19$:** A point $(x,y,z) \in (\mathbb{F}_p^\times)^3$ of odd multiplicative order on the CC1 curve for an odd prime $p \in \{3,5,7,11,13,17,19\}$ (refutes `T2d`).
5. **Differential mismatch between Rust and Python:** Disagreement on any exact integer invariant (`det_terms`, `det_const_term`, `det_augmentation`, `hensel_2adic_supports`, `haah_cc1_p23_L11_k`) or deviation $> 10^{-6}$ on Ronkin / braid invariants.
6. **Retracted or fabricated citation:** Any entry in `paper/references.bib` failing live Crossref DOI / arXiv resolution or flagged as retracted.
